# PHBox final-assembly BOM

Prepared 31 October 2026 for **one wider-layout unit**, approximately **292.6 × 191 × 17 mm**, excluding keycaps. Procurement preference: **Perth pickup first**, Australian shipped exact parts next, international only for unavailable exact parts. No orders or supplier inquiries have been sent.

This is a parts list for the intended finished assembly. **The current wider PCB is not ready to fabricate**: its hash-linked audit records 22 unconnected items. Heatsink screw length/retention, option-switch actuator height and socket MPN are unresolved. Hold the affected orders below. The October 6 Gerbers/BOM/CPL predate the present top-side C2344 USB-A and wider layout.

Quantities are installed quantities unless stated. Assumed configuration: **16 Choc V2 switches, 13 fitted 20 mm caps and 3 PLA blanking plugs**. Buy 16 caps if you want three spares or the option of exposing all controls. Confirm blanks do not preload any switches retained underneath.

The assembled-PCB row includes all fitted board electronics. The OLED, sockets and tactile switches are broken out to make sourcing visible, **not to add duplicate purchases**. If JLCPCB cannot supply/fit an item, purchase only the omitted quantity for hand assembly. Current USB1 is HRO-TYPE-C-31-M-12 / C165948; USB2 is Jing 902-131A1021D10100 / C2344, top-side through-hole. BOOTSEL/RESET use the existing board switches. RGB-strip expansion is not part of the saved design.

## Fabrication and assembly notes

- Layer order: top acrylic → upper spacer/plate/heatsink/screen → PCB → real foam → lower spacer → bottom acrylic. PCB and its attachments remain a floating foam-supported island.
- Use **individual current parts** linked above. The combined current assembly STEP still contains the earlier top cover; it is not cutting artwork. Top DXF is regenerated; bottom cover is supplied as STEP. DXF profiles use millimetres and zero kerf compensation. Have the shop compensate once.
- Covers are **2.0 mm cast acrylic**. Obtain a countersinking coupon before machining their 1.65 mm-deep countersinks. Do not substitute thicker sheet without revising the stack.
- The switch plate is **1.30 mm aluminium** and the heatsink is **milled 6061-T6 with contact bosses**. Neither is replaced by a plastic mock. There is one upper heatsink in the current design, with no lower aluminium plate.
- All stepped insulating posts, rear caps and relieved washers are custom geometry. Printed pilot holes and nominal CAD fits are not thread-strength validation. The existing plan permits printed prototypes; POM machining is an alternative. Ask a local plastics workshop for capability and a quote if printed joints fail qualification.
- Foam is real cushioning stock, not the rigid height gauges. Request small offcuts/samples rather than large rolls. Thermal interface pieces must be electrically insulating **thermal gap pads**; plain insulating washers are not a substitute.
- Case columns are separate from the insulating heatsink stops. O-rings are only an alternative to support washers after compressed-height testing; do not add them to the current stack.
- Standard A1 bed is smaller than the full-width panels. The supplied quartered PLA jobs are fit mocks. For a finished build, qualify section joints or quote a larger printer; do not treat an old mock stack as a final-material print job.
- Optional console authentication dongles depend on the target platform and are excluded from the base BOM. A laptop USB data cable is included. Additional LEDs, a bottom heatsink and extra option-button springs are excluded.
